Connect to Notion

Enter your Notion Internal Integration Token to load live trade data from your journals.

  1. Go to notion.so/profile/integrations
  2. Open your Trading Dashboard integration
  3. Copy the Internal Integration Token
  4. Make sure the integration is connected to all 3 journals in Notion
    (Open each journal → ··· → Connections → Trading Dashboard)
🔒 Token is saved in your browser only. Never sent to any third-party server.
