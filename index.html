Connect to Notion

To pull live trade data from your Notion journals, enter your Notion Internal Integration Token below. It's stored only in your browser — never sent anywhere else.

  1. Go to notion.so/profile/integrations
  2. Click + New integration
  3. Name it Trading Dashboard, click Save
  4. Copy the Internal Integration Token (starts with ntn_ or secret_)
  5. Make sure the integration is connected to your Trading Hub page in Notion (open page → ••• → Connections → Add)
