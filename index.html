Connect to Notion

Enter your Notion Internal Integration Token to pull live trade data from your journals.

  1. Go to notion.so/profile/integrations
  2. Click + New integration → name it Trading Dashboard → Save
  3. Copy the token shown (starts with ntn_)
  4. In Notion, open each of your 3 journals → click ··· top right → Connections → add Trading Dashboard
  5. Paste the token below and connect
⚠️ Your token is stored only in your browser's localStorage. It is never sent to any server other than Notion's official API.
